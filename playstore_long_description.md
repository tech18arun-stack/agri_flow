# Agri Flow | அக்ரி ப்ளோ
Connecting Soil to Soul: Tamil Nadu’s Premier Agricultural Marketplace

Agri Flow is a revolutionary digital ecosystem designed specifically for the agricultural landscape of Tamil Nadu. Whether you are a farmer looking for the best price, a merchant seeking quality volume, or a customer wanting farm-fresh produce, Agri Flow brings the marketplace to your fingertips.

---

### 🧠 Smart Mandi Price Engine
Say goodbye to guesswork. Our proprietary price engine analyzes real-time data across Tamil Nadu districts to give you:
- **Live Price Trends**: 7-day historical snapshots for all major crops.
- **AI Price Suggestions**: Farmers get data-driven recommendations when listing harvests.
- **Demand Indicators**: Real-time alerts for "High Demand" crops in your region.
- **District Comparison**: Compare prices across Coimbatore, Madurai, Salem, and more!

### 🌾 For Farmers: Empowerment in Every Listing
- **Direct Access**: Sell directly to verified merchants and customers.
- **Digital Records**: Track your revenue, active orders, and sales history effortlessly.
- **Weather Alerts**: Get district-specific weather advisory for optimal harvesting.
- **Bilingual Support**: Full control in both **English and தமிழ்**.

### 💼 For Merchants: Simplified Bulk Procurement
- **Letter of Intent (LOI)**: Send digital offers to farmers with one tap.
- **Smart Inventory**: Manage your purchased stock and track freshness.
- **Profit & Loss Tracking**: Integrated tools to monitor your margins and trading performance.
- **Verified Listings**: Direct transparency into the farm source and organic status.

### 🛒 For Customers: Farm-Fresh Transparency
- **Compare & Save**: Live price comparison across all active sellers.
- **Direct Source**: know exactly where your food comes from with district-wise tracking.
- **Quality Badges**: Look for "100% Certified Organic" and "Pesticide Free" labels.
- **Fast Delivery**: 24-hour delivery hubs across supported districts.
